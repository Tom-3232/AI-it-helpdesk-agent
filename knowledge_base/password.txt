=== Password Reset & Account Lockout Guide ===

Problem Overview:
User forgot domain/email password, account is locked out after too many invalid attempts, or single sign-on (SSO) fails.

Step 1: Self-Service Password Reset Portal (SSPR)
- Go to the company SSPR web page (e.g. https://password.company.com or https://passwordreset.microsoftonline.com).
- Enter your corporate email ID (e.g. user@company.com).
- Complete Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via Authenticator app SMS or push notification.
- Choose a new password adhering to corporate complexity requirements:
  - At least 12 characters
  - At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
  - At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
  - At least one number (0-9)
  - At least one special symbol (!@#$%^&*)
  - Cannot reuse your last 5 passwords.

Step 2: Account Lockout Auto-Unlock
- Standard active directory accounts lock automatically for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed attempts.
- Wait 15 minutes without typing credentials before trying again to allow the lockout timer to reset.

Step 3: Update Windows Credentials Manager
- If password was changed on web but laptop login rejects it while offline:
  - Connect laptop to corporate network via Ethernet or active VPN before logging in.
  - Open Windows Credentials Manager (Win + R -> `control /name Microsoft.CredentialManager`).
  - Clear stale stored web and Windows credentials.

Step 4: Contact IT Support Desk
- If SSPR is unavailable or MFA device is lost, open a support ticket to have an IT administrator trigger an administrative password reset or MFA token re-registration.
